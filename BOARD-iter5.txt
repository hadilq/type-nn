What we learned
===============

max_or / max_and are gone on every scale-* recipe. Spawn is dummy-rule
plus a Jacobian test. LN_WIDE still writes max_or only when growpol is
off (legacy ln-v2w / next).

  jac / ej / slack / sign   Almost never spawn on these UCI files.
                            Tiny nets (iris 60, iono 70). Best iris
                            hold (0.978) and best diabetes MSE, but
                            ionosphere stays at the frozen-width
                            floor (0.755). Conservative is not enough
                            width for the radar file.

  energy / mix              Grow Ors when the live set cannot sum to
                            the residual. Wine / wdbc / iris stay at
                            the type-nn hold with fewer params. Plain
                            energy on ionosphere explodes the product
                            (hold 0.500). Pairing with ln-v2w saves
                            it (0.877 / 0.887).

  keep                      Still the ionosphere winner among type-nn
                            (0.896, 560 params). Dummy-always-on with
                            prune is a scale policy, not a cap.

  layer / Ljac              Inserts on iris and kills hold (0.356).
                            Stuck+refuse is not a substitute for a
                            count cap: one identity hidden that
                            jitters off 1 never drops and wrecks the
                            map. Do not ship depth yet.

  ln-v2w+scale-mix          Best ln pairing: iono 0.887 at 281 params
                            and iris 0.978. Energy-on-Or is the axis
                            that ionosphere actually uses.

c-mlp still holds ionosphere (0.943) and wine (1.000). type-nn now
beats c-mlp on iris (0.978 vs 0.956) and wdbc (0.953 vs 0.936) with
no numeric width cap.

Next
  1. Ship scale-keep for raw product, ln-v2w+scale-mix for the ln
     tail. Both have no max_or / max_and.
  2. Depth is still the open problem. Ljac is too eager on easy
     files. Need a probe that cannot fire while a one-layer product
     is still cutting the residual across the epoch — without a clock.
  3. Still no clocks.
