type-nn-ln + Adam + assembly index
==================================

Split: xorshift32 Fisher-Yates, seed 34972, 70/30.

a_{i,r} is the assembly index of clause r on head i
in the type generating function

    z_i = Π_r A_{i,r}^{a_{i,r}} = S_i exp(Σ_r a_{i,r} log|A_{i,r}|)

Every ln-* recipe now forces a ≥ LN_A_MIN = 1e-4. A clause that
sits on the floor after the update is dropped (assembled to nothing);
a new dummy is born at a = 1.

Adam (β1=0.9, β2=0.999, ε=1e-8) on W, b, and a when LN_ADAM is on.
Step size is 0.1 × the bench SGD lr so the same 0.02–0.05 schedule
does not explode. Combine: ln-v2w+adam, ln-v2w+scale-mix+adam.

What we learned
===============

Adam on this sparse product is not a free lunch. The same recipe that
wins as SGD (ln-v2w, ln-v2w+scale-mix) loses hold-out once W/b/a move
with Adam, even at 0.1× lr: more params, more Or churn, worse acc.
The one file where Adam is less bad is wdbc (0.795 vs SGD 0.649 this
cut) — still well behind c-mlp 0.936.

Keep SGD as the ln default. ln-adam stays as a named control.
Assembly a>0 is on every ln-* recipe.
