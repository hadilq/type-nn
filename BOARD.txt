What the policy actually did (ladd)
  Every real file added one layer: a k=1 basis in front of the product
  once ge said the raw product was stalled. Width then grew only while
  ge stayed above 0.08 and out < in (full column rank of a linear map).
  No file used a hardcoded k=1/k=2/k=1 lock or a tick-16 commit.
